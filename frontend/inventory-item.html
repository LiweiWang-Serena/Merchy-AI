Turnover

Turnover is cost of goods sold divided by average stock value, measured at the end of each day. Days of inventory is the period length divided by turnover. Sell-through is units sold divided by opening stock plus units received. Sales pace only counts days the item was in stock, so a stockout doesn't make demand look lower than it is. Turnover spikes for an item that ran out of stock, because its average stock value was near zero.
