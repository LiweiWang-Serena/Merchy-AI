eBay account

Connect the store's eBay seller account to bring in its listings and orders. Read-only: nothing on eBay is changed.

Set up the connection

These settings are missing from backend/.env: .

  1. At developer.ebay.com, open Application Keys and create a keyset. Start with Sandbox, eBay's test environment; switch to Production once the store owner agrees to connect the real account.
  2. Copy the keyset's App ID into EBAY_CLIENT_ID and its Cert ID into EBAY_CLIENT_SECRET, and set EBAY_ENVIRONMENT=sandbox.
  3. On the keyset's User Tokens page, choose "Get a Token from eBay via Your Application" and add an eBay Redirect URL. Keep eBay's own confirmation page as the auth accepted URL (eBay only accepts HTTPS addresses, not localhost), turn on OAuth, and save. Copy the RuName it creates into EBAY_RUNAME.
  4. Restart the server (Ctrl+C, then python app.py) and reload this page.

To sign in to Sandbox you need a Sandbox test user, which you can create on the developer site's Sandbox user registration page. Sandbox accounts start with no listings or orders.

Connect the account

The account owner signs in on eBay and clicks Agree. Nobody shares a password, and the owner can withdraw access at any time.

Listings

Brings in each active listing's quantity and price by SKU. They appear on the Listings page and are checked against your stock.

Orders

Records paid eBay orders as sales in the inventory ledger. Orders already imported are skipped, so running it again is safe.

removes the saved authorization from this app; sales already imported stay in the ledger. To withdraw it on eBay's side too, the owner removes the app in their eBay account settings.

What's read, and what isn't

Listings: SKU, title, price and quantity available. Orders: order number, date, SKU, quantity, price and eBay's fees. Buyer names and addresses aren't stored, and nothing on eBay is changed.
