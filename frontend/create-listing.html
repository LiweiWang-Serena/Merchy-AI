New eBay listing from an Amazon link

Paste one of your own Amazon listing URLs to draft the matching eBay listing

How this works: this pulls the title, bullet points, and photos from a listing you already own on Amazon, and reuses them to draft the same product on eBay — it does not copy content from other sellers' or brands' listings. In production, the fetch step calls your Amazon Seller Central data (via the SP-API) for your own catalog.
